    Five engines, one parcel record

    Land, legal, GIS and research data sit in different places today. Bhumi-Netra unifies them, automates detection and analysis, tests policy impact before it is implemented, and turns records into decisions.

    From land data to evidence-based decisions

    Data moves up through five layers, like ground rising to the surface. Each layer only asks for what the one below it has already cleaned and connected.

    1. 1

      Data sources

      Land records, cadastral and GIS data, satellite imagery, legal, climate and socio-economic data.

    2. 2

      Unified land layer

      Bhumi-Dossier, APIs and ETL on PostgreSQL with PostGIS.

    3. 3

      AI intelligence

      RAG and NLP, a legal knowledge graph, computer vision and machine learning.

    4. 4

      Policy simulation

      Feasibility and risk, plus economic, environmental and social impact.

    5. 5

      Actionable output

      Alerts, risk and feasibility scores, policy insights and research intelligence.

    Built for pilots first, then scale

    Bhumi-Netra is designed for pilot validation, secure adoption and scale-up.

    Why it is feasible

    Modular and scalable
    Five modules are developed independently, then integrated.
    Data-ready
    Uses existing land, GIS, satellite and legal datasets.
    Proven tech
    AI and ML, RAG, GIS, remote sensing and cloud.
    Pilot to scale
    A district or state pilot, then validation, then national rollout.
    Human-in-the-loop
    Officials validate critical AI-assisted decisions.

    Risks and the safeguards that answer them

    Choose a risk to see which safeguards answer it.

    • Standardized data Common schemas, validation and provenance.
    • Secure architecture RBAC, encryption, audit logs and controlled APIs.
    • Human validation Critical AI alerts are reviewed before action.
    • Confidence-aware AI Confidence scores and source evidence.
    • Pilot, validate, scale Historical and limited data testing before deployment.
    • API-first Connect existing systems without replacing them.
